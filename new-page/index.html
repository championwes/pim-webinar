Webinar Agenda

What We'll Cover

The same session that packed the room at PWX Houston, in a tight 55 minutes you can watch without leaving your desk.

  1. Welcome & Introductions – Why street ponding is more than a nuisance.
  2. The Real Cost of Ponding – Advanced degradation, accessibility barriers, hydroplaning, public complaints, and lower property values.
  3. Why Traditional Fixes Fall Short – Mill & fill, replacement, and mud jacking cost $75–$200 per linear foot, and often just relocate the problem.
  4. The Precision Flowline Method – Pre-feasibility, topography analysis, repair, and flow testing.
  5. Case Study Spotlight – Before-and-after results on roadways, cul-de-sacs, intersections, and pedestrian access routes.
  6. Building a Program – Find drainage problems during resurfacing, ADA, and right-of-way assessments, before complaints arrive.
  7. Q&A – Live audience questions answered by our expert.
